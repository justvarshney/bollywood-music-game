📖 How to setup MSG91 WhatsApp OTP in 2 minutes:
  1. Log in to your MSG91 Panel and navigate to the OTP section.
  2. Click Create New Widget (or Edit your existing Widget) and choose Verification Type: Login using OTP.
  3. Under Primary Channel, select WhatsApp (with SMS fallback).
  4. Important for Direct In-App OTP: In your Widget Settings, toggle OFF "Google re-CAPTCHA validation" so that OTPs can be sent directly from the custom arcade screen without a captcha block. (If kept ON, players can use the "Verify with MSG91 OTP Widget" popup button).
